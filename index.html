About us

What is ΔBD Family Web?

ΔBD Family Web lets you build your family tree as a web. Every person is a point, every connection is a thread, and every family or clan has its own crest. Your family, friends and their relations all join into one connected web, which you can explore like a galaxy.

How it works

Home: The home page is a galaxy of family webs. Move around by dragging, scrolling or using the arrow keys. Zoom with Ctrl + scroll, pinch, the + and − buttons (hold them to keep zooming), or the + and − keys. Press 0 or Reset to return to the start.

Space: Tap a planet, moon, exoplanet, galaxy or black hole to fly there. Zoom in close to see its name and its surface.

Clans: Each clan you create appears in the galaxy with its own crest. Tap a clan to see its members and the clans related to it.

Search: Use the search bar at the top of the home page to find a clan, a family name or a person.

Projects: Press + Add new project to start a project. Each project has its own family tree. Use the ⋮ menu on a project to rename, save, share a read-only link or delete it. Anyone with the link can view the shared project.

Family tree: The first person you add is you (me). Add family and friends, then connect each one to people already in the web and choose how they are related: Father, Mother, Sibling, Friend, Spouse, Child or Other. When you add a child to someone who has a spouse, the child is linked to both parents automatically, and a new spouse is linked to the partner's children too. Each clan has its own ring colour (up to 60 different colours). Imported files are arranged automatically, one row per generation. Press the arrange icon at the top to tidy any web again, one row per generation. Tap a person to see their details, add a relative, delete or modify them. Drag a person to move them, and drag the background to look around.

Undo and redo: Use the arrows at the top of the family tree page, or Ctrl + Z and Ctrl + Y, to step back and forward through added, edited, deleted or moved people.

Clan view: Press the galaxy button on the family tree page to see that project's clans as crests in space. Tap a clan to see its members in generation order.

Languages: Press the globe icon to show names and labels in English, Tamil, Malayalam, Arabic, Hindi, Japanese or Mandarin.

Full screen: Press the corner button at the bottom left of the home page or the family tree page to hide the tools and see only the web. Press it again, or Esc, to bring them back.

Help: Press the ? icon for a step by step guide with a small practice model you can try.

Relatives: Give each person a first name, last name, clan, gender (male or female), status (living or deceased), and an optional photo from your gallery or files.

Settings: Press the gear icon on the projects page to export all your data as CSV or JSON, or to import a file. An imported file adds its people to the project named in the file.
