About us

What is ΔBD Family Web?

ΔBD Family Web lets you build your family tree as a web. Every person is a point, every connection is a thread, and every clan has its own crest. Your family, friends and their relations join into one connected web, which you can explore like a galaxy.

Home and space

Move around: Drag, scroll, or use the arrow keys (hold Shift to move faster). Zoom with Ctrl + scroll, pinch, the + and − buttons (hold them to keep zooming), or the + and − keys. Press 0 or Reset to go back to the start.

Space: The Sun, the eight planets with their moons, asteroids, comets, exoplanets, black holes, galaxies and star clusters are all on the map. Zoom in to see names and each object's surface. Tap an object to fly to it.

Clans: Each clan you create appears in the galaxy with its own crest. Tap a clan crest to see its members in generation order, and the clans related to it.

Full screen: The button at the bottom left hides the buttons and text, so you see only the webs. Press it again to show them.

Search: Use the search bar to find a clan, a family name or a person.

Projects

Press Projects to open your projects. Each project has its own family tree and its own galaxy.

Use the ⋮ menu on a project to Rename it (title and description), Save, Share a link, or Delete it. Delete asks you to confirm first.

Clan view: Press Clan view on a project card, or the galaxy button at the bottom right of its family tree, to open that project's own galaxy. Each project's galaxy has its own shape (spiral, barred spiral, elliptical or irregular) and colours. Its clans are shown as crests, joined by threads. Zoom and drag to look around, and tap a crest to see that clan's members, oldest generation first.

Family tree

Start: The first person you add is you (me). Give each person a first and last name, clan, gender (male or female), status (living or deceased), and optionally a date of birth and a photo from your gallery or files.

Connect people: Every new person must be connected to at least one person already in the tree. Choose the relation: Father, Mother, Spouse, Child, Sibling, Friend or Other. Use Other to describe your own relation.

Add relative: Tap a person, then Add relative, and choose Parents, Spouse, Child, Sibling or Friend. The form opens with that relation already selected.

Relatives list: A person's card lists their relatives in this order: Father, Mother, Spouse, Child, Sibling, then Friend and Other. Repeats are numbered, for example "1st spouse" and "2nd spouse". Scroll the list to see everyone.

Move and look around: Drag a person to move them, or drag the background. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Imported people are arranged in generation rows, so family groups stay together.

Modify and delete: Tap a person, then Modify to change their details or connections. Delete asks you to confirm before removing them.

Undo and redo: The buttons at the top right of the family tree undo and redo your changes. On a computer, Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y do the same.

Languages

Press the language button on the family tree to switch between English, Tamil, Malayalam, Arabic, Hindi, Japanese and Mandarin. Buttons, relation names and clan words change with it. Names are shown in the language you choose if you have added a translation for them; otherwise the name you typed is shown. Add translations with Names in other languages in the person form.

Settings, sharing and saving

Export and import: The gear icon on the projects page exports all your projects as CSV or JSON, and imports a file back. Importing adds each person to the project named in the file.

Share: The Share option in a project's ⋮ menu creates a link. Anyone signed in who opens it sees the project read-only. Sharing needs the page to be opened on claude.ai.

Phone: The phone's back button closes the open popup or goes back one screen. Swiping right from the left edge of the screen does the same. On the home page, back stays in the app.

Good to know: Your projects, family members and clans are kept while this page is open. Export your data from Settings to keep a copy, and import it again later to restore it.
